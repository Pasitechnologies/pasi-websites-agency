Have a question? These quick notes may help you prepare for a conversation with PASI.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

A short description of your business, what you want the website to do and any timing or features you already have in mind is a helpful start.

Can I request a project estimate?

Yes. Use the request a quote form to outline your project needs. It currently offers local validation only and does not transmit or store information.

Is the contact form live?

Not yet. The form checks your entries in the browser, but backend submission is planned for a later development phase. Please do not enter sensitive information.

Where is PASI Website Agency based?

PASI Website Agency is based in Uganda and aims to work with businesses locally and internationally.
