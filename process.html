HomeOur Process

Five clear stages

From first conversation
to launch day.

Our process creates space for good questions, clear decisions and a result that feels right for your business.

  1. 01
    LISTEN & UNDERSTAND

    Discovery

    We start by understanding your business, target audience and objectives. We'll discuss what you want the website to achieve, what makes your business distinct and what a successful result means to you.

    Your part: Share your goals, ideas and what you know about your customers.
  2. 02
    SHAPE THE PLAN

    Planning

    Together, we determine the website structure, required features, content needs and development strategy. A clear plan helps us align on scope and priorities before visual design begins.

    Your part: Review the proposed structure and tell us what matters most.
  3. 03
    MAKE IT FEEL RIGHT

    Design

    We create the visual design and user experience around your brand and audience. We share the direction for your feedback and refine it collaboratively before moving into development.

    Your part: Give thoughtful feedback so the design reflects your vision.
  4. 04
    BUILD & REFINE

    Development

    Once the design is approved, we build it into a functional, responsive website using modern web technologies. We check the experience across devices and refine details as we go.

    Your part: Provide agreed content and review progress at key points.
  5. 05
    READY FOR THE WORLD

    Launch

    We test key pages, links and responsive layouts, make final refinements and publish the completed website once everything is approved. We'll help you understand the handover and next steps.

    Your part: Give final approval and celebrate your new digital home.
