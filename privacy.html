Your privacy matters.

This page explains what information may be involved when you visit the PASI Website Agency website and what choices are available to you.

HomePrivacy policy
Last reviewed: 27 November 2026

This policy describes the current Phase 2 frontend. Contact and quote forms currently validate in your browser only and do not send or store submissions.

Questions about privacy? Contact us

1. Information this website may collect

When you browse this website, your browser and hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver pages, such as a request timestamp, requested page, browser type and network information. The website itself does not currently include analytics or an account system.

If you type information into the contact or quote forms, it is checked locally in your browser. In this phase, the forms do not submit that information to PASI or store it on a server. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential details.

2. How information may be used

Information that you choose to send through a confirmed PASI communication channel may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand a potential project, and communicate about services. Any future online form collection will require a review of the form's data handling and an update to this policy before it is enabled.

3. Contact form information

The present contact and quotation forms are frontend demonstrations only. Their validation helps identify missing or incorrectly formatted fields, but a successful local check is not a message submission. No success notice should be interpreted as PASI having received your information.

4. Data protection and retention

Where PASI receives information through a communication channel, it will be handled with reasonable care and used for the purpose for which it was provided. The specific storage, access and retention practices for business enquiries will be documented when the corresponding backend process is implemented. No security certification or legal compliance claim is made by this policy.

5. Third-party services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, icons from Font Awesome's public CDN and illustrative images from Unsplash. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser retrieves their resources. Their services are subject to their own privacy notices and terms. If external resources are unavailable, some visual details may not display.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The current frontend does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Your hosting provider, browser or third-party assets may use technical mechanisms under their own services. This policy should be reviewed if cookies or analytics are introduced.

7. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use the enquiry forms, limit information entered into them, or contact PASI through confirmed contact details when those are published. You may ask what information PASI has received from you or request an appropriate correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements and the practical records available.

8. Contact and updates

Official business email and phone details have not yet been provided for this website. Please use the contact page to see available contact options as they are confirmed. PASI may revise this policy as the website and its services develop; the review date above should be updated when material changes are made.
